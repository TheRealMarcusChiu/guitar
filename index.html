About this piece

The form holds one pose until sound arrives. Low notes widen and sway the silk, the middle of the spectrum shapes each fold, high detail runs along the threads, and loudness sets the light. The string carries the waveform itself. A recording with no sound leaves everything at rest, and the picture stays hidden.
